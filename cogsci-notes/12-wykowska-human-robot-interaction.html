Social cognition in human-robot interaction

Speaker: Agnieszka Wykowska

Wykowska introduced herself as a principal investigator at the Italian Institute of Technology, working on human-robot interaction and social cognition. Her group studies how people attribute intentionality and socially attune to robots and other embodied artificial agents. She mentioned holding a Hans Fischer Senior Fellowship at the Institute for Advanced Study at the Technical University of Munich, and was dialing in from Munich for the talk.

She joked that she felt like "the odd one out" in this particular session, since her work is on embodied systems, robots people actually share physical space with, rather than the disembodied, text-based systems most of the rest of the program had been about.
