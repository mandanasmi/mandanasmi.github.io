CogSci 2026 — Talk Notes

Notes from talks, prize lectures, and one workshop panel. Click through to any of them below.

A caveat on all of these: I took these notes live (or wrote them up shortly after from memory), so there's a decent chance I misheard a name, or got a number slightly off somewhere. Treat the gist as reliable and the fine details, especially exact attributions in the discussions, with a bit of skepticism.

  • Do multilingual models and brains share a format?Andrea Gregor de Varda — shared units across languages, and a 21-language brain-alignment study
  • Poverty, income instability, and child cognitive developmentJulia Hermida — deficit vs. adaptation, the maternal-migration reversal, rural vs. urban Argentina
  • Sacred and secular values in how we see natureSonya Sachdeva — the Ganges studies, Jamaica Bay, fire suppression, and 400,000 public comments
  • A resource-rational model of memory-guided deliberationMarcelo Mattar — eye tracking, "looking at nothing," and a meta-RL model of memory sampling
  • Poster follow-up: the memory-sampling model, in more depthJonathan Nicholas — the same project, a more technical Q&A
  • What working memory limits mean for planningFred Calloway — decision trees, forgetting, and whether representation is just attention

Prize lectures
